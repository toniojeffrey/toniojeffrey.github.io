Campaign Strategy

Simple, controlled testing for a local business

The mock campaign was structured to compare audience approach and creative angle while keeping the campaign goal focused on Messenger conversations.

Campaign Goal

Generate qualified local inquiries and potential orders through Messenger.

  • Objective: Engagement / Messaging
  • Destination: Facebook Messenger
  • Performance goal: Maximize conversations
  • Bid strategy: Highest volume
  • Advantage+ placements

Testing Structure

Both ad sets use the same creatives and budget so the audience approach can be compared more fairly.

  • Ad Set 1: Broad local audience
  • Ad Set 2: Food-interest audience
  • Creative 1: Product / craving angle
  • Creative 2: Celebration / bilao angle
  • ₱160 daily budget per ad set
Transparency note: This campaign setup is a current Meta Ads workflow demonstration created for my portfolio. It was saved as a draft and not published, so no mock results are presented as real performance.

Creative Strategy

Two ad angles for different buying moments

The creatives were designed to test an everyday product/craving angle against an occasion-based bilao angle.

Ad Creative 01

Freshly Made Kakanin

Product-focused angle for merienda, sharing, and everyday cravings. CTA: Message to Order.

Ad Creative 02

May Celebration?

Occasion-focused angle for birthdays, meetings, and family gatherings. CTA: Reserve Your Order.
